Wind Waker HD VR Mod: The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD in virtual reality

VR-WakerHD is a free, open-source Wind Waker VR mod: a VR version of The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD. You play in first person through Link's eyes, with head tracking, motion controls, your arms as Link's arms and a sword you swing yourself. It runs on PC VR headsets through OpenXR (Windows and Linux) and standalone on Meta Quest 3 and 3S. It is built on ZeldaWWHDRecomp, the native port (recompilation) of the Wii U game.

Unofficial, and no game included. VR-WakerHD is a fan-made project, not made by or affiliated with Nintendo. You need your own, legally obtained copy of the Wii U game: setup builds the game on your own device from your own game files. The downloads contain no game code, game data or keys.

Gameplay tutorial

The Legend of Zelda Wind Waker HD VR: (VR-WakerHD) Tutorial + Gameplay! #1 was made by legaiaflame, who also tests VR-WakerHD and reports its bugs. The 52-minute video opens with a four-minute tutorial that shows every VR control in the headset, then plays VR-WakerHD from the Great Sea through Dragon Roost Cavern, the Forbidden Woods and the Tower of the Gods to the Master Sword, with real sword fights, boss battles and sailing.

What the tutorial teaches

In order (watch from 0:12):

  • Settings: click the left stick for the VR options menu (sliders, mods and cheats).
  • Views: click the right stick to switch between first person and third person.
  • The HUD on your wrists: turn your hands over to see your hearts and rupees.
  • Ducking and crawling: push the right stick down.
  • Sword: reach behind your right shoulder, hold the right grip and pull forward to draw it, then swing it yourself.
  • Shield: raise it to block attacks.
  • Lock-on: the left trigger targets an enemy. Hold and release B (Circle) for a spin attack.
  • Item wheel: hold the right trigger, pick an item with the right stick and let go to bring it out. (In the video, reaching over the left shoulder with the grip brings back the last item; now it opens the item wheel too.)
  • Bow: hold the left grip to pull the string, release to shoot.
  • Bombs: throw them with a forward motion of your hand.
  • Hookshot and boomerang: aim by pointing your hand, fire with a trigger.
  • Wind Waker: wait a second, then conduct with your right hand (left, right, up, down, middle, or hold the right trigger); move your left hand to the left to change the beat.
  • Sailing (4:05): the four boat views, then sailing at full speed.

What the Wind Waker VR mod does

  • First person at Link's eyes, in stereo 3D with 6DoF head tracking; third person is a stick click away. All features
  • Your arms are Link's arms: his hands follow your controllers, with natural elbows and shoulders.
  • Physical sword: draw it over your shoulder and swing it. The bow, the Skull Hammer and the Wind Waker baton are motion-controlled too.
  • Item wheel at your shoulder and the HUD on Link's wrists; menus and the GamePad screen on panels in the headset.
  • True size (new in 0.6.0): play at your own height instead of Link's.
  • Comfort and performance: smooth or snap turning, multiview, foveated rendering on Quest, dynamic resolution and upscaling.

Get started

  1. Download the file for your platform from the latest release.
  2. Start it and choose your own game files; setup builds the game on your device (once).
  3. Press Play VR in the VR launcher.

Project status
