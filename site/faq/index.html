Wind Waker VR mod: FAQ and troubleshooting

Common questions about VR-WakerHD, the Wind Waker HD VR mod, and fixes for common problems.

Questions

Is there a Wind Waker VR mod?
Yes. VR-WakerHD is a free, open-source VR mod of The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD: first person through Link's eyes with head tracking, motion controls and a sword you swing yourself, on PC VR and Meta Quest 3 / 3S.
Is VR-WakerHD official?
No. It is a fan project, not made, endorsed or sponsored by Nintendo, or by the ZeldaWWHDRecomp authors.
Does it include the game?
No. You need your own, legally obtained copy of the Wii U game. Setup builds it on your device from your own files, and what it makes must not be shared.
Is it an emulator or a VR injector like VorpX?
Neither. It builds on ZeldaWWHDRecomp, which recompiles the game's code to native code, and the VR is built into the renderer and the game's own logic.
Can I play Wind Waker VR on Quest 3 without a PC?
Yes, standalone on Quest 3 and 3S with the APK. Quest 2 and Quest Pro are not tested.
Does it work on Linux?
Yes: the Linux AppImage, with SteamVR, Monado or WiVRn.
Which headsets work?
Any headset with an OpenXR runtime that supports Vulkan. PlayStation VR2 and Meta Quest 3 / 3S are tested most.
Is it free and open source?
Yes. The VR mode is GPL-3.0-or-later with a credit term, and the original port's code is MPL-2.0.
Can I play without a headset?
Yes. Play Flat in the launcher plays the base port on the monitor.
What was VR-WakerHD called before?
ZeldaWWHD-VR.

Playing

  • Settings in the headset: click the left stick; the grips switch tabs.
  • The sword: reach over your right shoulder, press the grip to draw it, and swing it fully to attack.
  • First and third person: click the right stick.
  • The view is off-centre: recentre with both stick clicks.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to check
VR does not startAn OpenXR runtime must be installed and active; the log's [vr] lines say why
Low frame rateLower the headset resolution and field of view; on Quest see the Quest guide
Stutter on the first visit to an areaShaders are being made; it eases as you play
Wireless lags or stuttersTurn on Streaming optimal, try 90 Hz with Capped 45 fps, or use a cable
Hands driftRecentre (both stick clicks)
cannot load game/code/cking.rpxThe game folder must hold code, content and meta
