Wind Waker HD VR: The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD in virtual reality

VR-WakerHD is an unofficial, open-source VR version of The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD. You play in first person through Link's eyes, with head tracking, motion controls, your arms as Link's arms and a sword you swing yourself. It runs on PC VR headsets through OpenXR and standalone on Meta Quest 3 and 3S. It is built on ZeldaWWHDRecomp, the native port (recompilation) of the Wii U game.

No game included, and not affiliated with Nintendo. You need your own, legally obtained copy of the Wii U game. Setup builds the game on your own device from your own game files; the downloads contain no game code, data or keys.

A real Wind Waker VR port, not a flat screen in a headset

  • First person at Link's eyes, in stereo 3D with 6DoF head tracking and leaning; third person is a stick click away.
  • Your arms are Link's arms: his hands follow your motion controllers, with natural elbows and shoulders.
  • Physical sword: draw it over your shoulder and swing it; the bow, the Skull Hammer and the Wind Waker baton are motion-controlled too.
  • Item wheel at your shoulder and the HUD on Link's wrists; menus and the GamePad screen on panels in the headset.
  • Comfort and performance: smooth or snap turning, steady hands, multiview, foveated rendering on Quest, dynamic resolution and upscaling.

How it relates to ZeldaWWHDRecomp

ZeldaWWHDRecomp recompiles the Wii U game's code so it runs natively, flat-screen, on PC, Linux, macOS and Android. VR-WakerHD is a separate project built on that port: it adds the OpenXR VR mode, the first-person body, the motion controls, the VR launcher and the Quest 3 build. It is not an emulator and not a VR injector.

Questions
