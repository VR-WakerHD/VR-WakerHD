Wind Waker VR mod: playing The Wind Waker HD in VR

If you are looking for a Wind Waker VR mod, VR-WakerHD is one you can play today: The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD in VR, from start to finish, in first person with motion controls. It is free, open source and fan-made.

Unofficial, and no game included. VR-WakerHD is a fan-made project, not made by or affiliated with Nintendo. You need your own, legally obtained copy of the Wii U game: setup builds the game on your own device from your own game files. The downloads contain no game code, game data or keys.

What makes it a VR version, not a screen in a headset

VR-WakerHD isn't an emulator, and it isn't a VR injector like VorpX. It builds on ZeldaWWHDRecomp, which recompiles the Wii U game's code so it runs natively. The VR is built into the renderer and the game's own logic, which is how Link's arms can follow your controllers, his HUD can sit on his wrists, and your sword swings can be his attacks.

  • Stereo 3D with 6DoF head tracking, at Link's eyes or at your own height (True size).
  • Motion controls through OpenXR: the sword, the bow (with fire, ice and light arrows), the Skull Hammer, the Wind Waker baton, the boomerang and the grappling hook.
  • The GamePad screen, menus and dialogue on panels in the headset; hearts and rupees on Link's wrists.
  • Four views while sailing on the Great Sea.

Other Wind Waker VR projects

Earlier Wind Waker VR efforts ran the GameCube game in an emulator: a 2014 Oculus Rift video made with Dolphin, and DolphinXR, a Dolphin build with VR patches for several games. VR-WakerHD is a different approach: the Wii U HD version, natively recompiled, with the VR mode written for this one game.

What you need

  • Your own copy of the Wii U game (a disc image with its keys, a Cemu .wua, or an extracted game folder).
  • Either a PC VR headset with an OpenXR runtime and a Vulkan GPU, or a Meta Quest 3 / 3S in developer mode.
