Wind Waker VR on Meta Quest 3 and 3S

VR-WakerHD runs The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD in VR standalone on Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S, with no PC. A Quest can also play the PC version over Quest Link or Virtual Desktop: see PC VR. Quest 2 and Quest Pro are not tested.

Unofficial, and no game included. VR-WakerHD is a fan-made project, not made by or affiliated with Nintendo. You need your own, legally obtained copy of the Wii U game: setup builds the game on your own device from your own game files. The downloads contain no game code, game data or keys.

Install on the Quest

  1. Put the headset in developer mode (a Meta developer account, then the setting in the Meta Horizon app).
  2. Install VR-WakerHD-Quest3-0.6.0.apk from the latest release with SideQuest or adb install.
  3. Open it from Unknown sources and choose your game files. The app offers to extract a disc image; setup compiles the game on the headset, which takes a while the first time.

Recommended settings

The Quest's processor is the limit, so the defaults are tuned for it:

SettingDefault on QuestIf it is not smooth
Refresh rate and frame rate90 Hz, Capped 30 fps72 Hz with 36 fps and frame synthesis on
Headset resolution0.70Lower it (for example 0.65)
Field of viewFullLower it: this helps the frame rate most
UpscalingSharpening (CAS) with edge smoothing (FXAA)—
Foveated renderingOn—
Frame synthesisAutoOff if the picture looks smeary or jittery
Shader compileOffFast can reduce stutter on first visits

Change them in the VR launcher or in the headset: click the left stick, then use the grips to switch tabs.

Low frame rate or stutter

  • The first visit to an area stutters while its shaders are made; it eases as you play.
  • Low frame rate throughout: lower the field of view and the headset resolution, and try 72 Hz with 36 fps.
  • Hands look out of place at low frame rates: raising the frame rate is the fix.

Bug reports
