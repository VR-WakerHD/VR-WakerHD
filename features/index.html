Wind Waker VR features

Everything VR-WakerHD adds to The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD, as of version 0.6.0. Features marked experimental are new and less tested.

First person and head tracking

  • The view at Link's eyes in stereo 3D, with full 6DoF head tracking. Leaning is followed, and the world is scaled to Link's arms.
  • Link turns to face where you look when you stand still; walking, he faces where he walks.
  • True size (experimental): your eyes at your real height in his world, with only his hands drawn, moving freely with your controllers.
  • Third person (right stick click) uses the game's own camera, turned by your head.
  • Sailing views on the Great Sea and in the Tower of the Gods.

Motion controls

  • Your arms are Link's arms: inverse kinematics with natural elbows and shoulders.
  • Physical sword: draw it over your shoulder; full swings and thrusts play the game's attacks, the blade hits along its tracked path, with hit vibration.
  • Combat: lock-on with the trigger, automatic parry, and a shield that guards while locked on.
  • Items in your hands: the bow (pull the string with your drawing hand; swap fire, ice and light arrows), the Skull Hammer, the Wind Waker baton (conduct with your hand), the boomerang and grappling hook, the telescope and the Picto Box.
  • Item wheel at your shoulder: reach over your shield shoulder, choose with the stick, pull the item out.
  • Left-handed play swaps the sword and shield hands.

Interface in VR

  • Wrist HUD: hearts, items, rupees and magic on Link's wrists.
  • Menus on panels: pause, map, bag and the GamePad screen, in front of you.
  • In-headset settings menu (left stick click) and a VR launcher on Windows, Linux, SteamOS and Quest.

Comfort

  • Smooth or snap turning, and Move where you look.
  • Steady hands while turning; depth of field, bloom and ambient occlusion can be turned off.

Graphics and performance

  • Multiview (both eyes in one pass) and foveated rendering on Quest.
  • Headset resolution, dynamic resolution and upscalers: Quest Super Resolution, SGSR 1 and 2, FSR 1, and sharpening with edge smoothing.
  • Frame rate choices: refresh-rate selection, capped modes and frame synthesis; Streaming optimal for Virtual Desktop and Air Link (experimental).
